Privacy Policy — Islamic Games Together

Effective Date: 2026-06-27

This Privacy Policy applies to the Islamic Games Together application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application") for mobile devices, developed by Onur Demircan (hereinafter referred to as the "Service Provider") as a Freemium service. This service is provided "AS IS".

Information the Application Collects

1.1 No Registration Required

The Application does not require account creation or registration. Players may use the Application immediately upon installation. The Service Provider does not collect names, email addresses, or any personally identifying information during onboarding.

1.2 Multiplayer Networking

The Application uses Photon Fusion 2 (provided by Exit Games GmbH) to enable real-time online multiplayer functionality. When a player participates in an online session, the following technical data is transmitted to Photon's servers solely for the purpose of establishing and maintaining the connection:

  • A temporary display name entered by the player within the Application (not linked to any account or identity)
  • The device's IP address, required for network routing
  • In-session game state data necessary to synchronize gameplay between players

This data is transient and is not retained beyond the active session. Photon's privacy policy governs data processed by their infrastructure and is available at: https://www.photonengine.com/en/PrivacyPolicy

1.3 Analytics (Optional — Consent-Based)

The Application integrates Unity Analytics (provided by Unity Technologies). Analytics collection is entirely optional and remains inactive unless the player explicitly grants consent through the in-app data-sharing setting. Consent may be withdrawn at any time via the Application's settings menu.

When consent is granted, the Application collects anonymized, aggregated gameplay data for the sole purpose of improving the Application. This includes information such as the device's system language, the selected in-app language, which game modes are played, gameplay configuration settings (such as round count, player count, and time limits), and which word categories are enabled. None of this data contains or is linked to any personally identifiable information.

Unity's privacy policy is available at: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

1.4 In-App Purchases

The Application offers non-consumable in-app purchases (such as word packs and player-limit upgrades) processed entirely through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. The Service Provider does not collect, process, or store any payment or billing information. All transactions are governed by the respective platform's terms and privacy policy:

  • Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchase entitlement status is stored locally on the device and verified through the relevant platform.

1.5 Automatically Collected Technical Data

Certain basic technical information may be collected automatically as part of standard platform operation, including device type and operating system version. This information is used solely to ensure application compatibility and is not shared for advertising purposes.

Location Data

The Application does not collect or use precise real-time location information from the device.

Artificial Intelligence

The Application does not employ Artificial Intelligence or machine learning technologies to process user data or deliver its features.

Third-Party Services

The Application incorporates the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Photon Fusion 2 (Exit Games GmbH) — Real-time multiplayer networking https://www.photonengine.com/en/PrivacyPolicy
  • Unity Analytics — Consent-based gameplay analytics https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Apple App Store — In-app purchase processing (iOS) https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google Play Store — In-app purchase processing (Android) https://policies.google.com/privacy

The Service Provider may disclose information when required by law, such as in response to a subpoena or similar legal process, or when disclosure is reasonably necessary to protect the rights and safety of users or the public.

Children's Privacy

The Application is designed to be family-friendly and is rated Ages 3+ on Google Play and Ages 4+ on the Apple App Store. Age ratings may vary by country in accordance with local store policies.

The Application does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13, or the applicable minimum age in the user's jurisdiction. The Application includes no registration mechanism, social profiles, or any means by which a child could submit personal information.

Should the Service Provider become aware that a child under the age of 13 has provided personal information, such data will be deleted promptly. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided personal information are encouraged to contact the Service Provider at gravityvortexstudios@gmail.com.

Your Choices and Opt-Out Rights

Players retain the following controls over data collection:

  • Analytics consent may be declined or revoked at any time through the Application's settings menu. Upon revocation, all analytics collection ceases immediately.
  • The Application may be uninstalled at any time to halt all data collection. Standard uninstall processes provided by the device or marketplace apply.
  • Multiplayer networking is only active when the player actively joins or hosts an online session.

Data Retention

  • Anonymized analytics data, if consent is granted, is retained by Unity in accordance with their own data retention policies.
  • Purchase entitlement data is managed by Apple or Google. The Application retains only a local record of ownership status on the device.
  • Multiplayer session data processed by Photon is transient and not retained beyond the active session.
  • Local settings and consent preferences are stored on the device and are removed upon uninstalling the Application.

Requests for deletion of any data held by the Service Provider may be directed to gravityvortexstudios@gmail.com and will be addressed within a reasonable timeframe.

Security

The Service Provider implements reasonable technical and procedural measures to safeguard information processed through the Application. However, no electronic system can guarantee absolute security, and the Service Provider cannot warrant that data will be free from all potential breaches.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the Application following the posting of changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.

Consent

By using the Application, you consent to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy, as amended from time to time.
